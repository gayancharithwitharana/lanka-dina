# Glossary

The words Lankadina uses for its domain terms in English, Sinhala and Tamil. `src/locales/si.json` and `ta.json` must use these terms, and only these, so the app reads consistently.

## How to review

- Correct any term in place, and change that language's status to `Reviewed`.
- Tamil should follow Sri Lankan usage, not Tamil Nadu usage.
- **Source** says where a term should come from:
  - **Gazette:** must match the wording of the official public holiday gazette.
  - **Cultural Affairs:** must match the Department of Cultural Affairs Avurudu nakath list.
  - **Native review:** everyday or standard vocabulary. A native speaker's review is enough.
  - **Needs source:** I'm unsure of the term. Confirm it against a trusted almanac or the Department of Cultural Affairs, then change the source.
- Every term starts as `Draft`, proposed by Claude and not yet checked.

Not in this glossary:
- Weekday and month names. They come from the phone's date formatting.
- Holiday names and individual nakath names, for example "Il Full Moon Poya" or "Lighting the hearth". They come from the official data files and are translated there.

Lankadina privacy policy

Last updated Nov 28, 2026

Lankadina is a calendar app for Sri Lankan holidays, Poya days and auspicious times. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This page explains what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and who receives it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your notes and reminders. They are stored only on your device.
  • Your settings. Language, week start, appearance and lagna are stored only on your device.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. The app doesn't use push notifications.

We can't see any of this. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app downloads

The app downloads updated holiday and nakath data from a public page hosted on GitHub Pages. The request contains no information about you, but GitHub, like any web host, receives your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Ads

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Calendar, Upcoming and Nakath screens. Google's ads software in the app collects and uses data to deliver and measure ads:

  • Your device's advertising ID and IP address
  • Basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice before ads load. On iPhone, the app also asks permission before your activity is used for personalized ads. Read how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Sharing

When you share a nakath list, the app creates an image or text and hands it to the app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you start it.

Children

Lankadina is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
